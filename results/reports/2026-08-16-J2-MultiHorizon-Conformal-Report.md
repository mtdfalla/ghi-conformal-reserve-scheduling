## What J2 adds beyond the conference (P3)

P3 established static split-conformal (ICP, ICP-norm, Mondrian, CQR). J2 adds, across **all** horizons:

1. **Mondrian-CQR** — regime-conditional Conformalized Quantile Regression: the two P3 winners fused (Mondrian's per-regime calibration + CQR's adaptive sharpness/CRPS).
2. **ACI** — Adaptive Conformal Inference (Gibbs & Candès 2021), online over 2024, with a per-day miscoverage reset to prevent interval inflation across the diurnal night-zeros.
3. **ACI-regime** — regime-conditional ACI (independent α_t and score pool per weather regime).
4. **Reliability-over-time** — monthly coverage across the 2024 test year, plus an ACI learning-rate (γ) sweep.

Code: `03_code/conformal/conformal_adaptive.py`, `03_code/_j2_one_horizon.py`, `03_code/_j2_aggregate.py`.

## Verdict (J2 verification criteria)

- **Coverage guarantees hold.** Every method is at or above 0.8732 marginal PICP at the
  90 % nominal level; ICP sits lowest, which is the test-year drift ACI corrects.
- **Mondrian-CQR beats each parent on the parent's weak axis.** It beats CQR on 5-min
  regime ACE-RMS (0.0081 against 0.0308) and Mondrian on CRPS (19.831 against 22.292).
  → adopted as the recommended interval method.
- **ACI tracks under drift.** Monthly PICP stays within [0.9001, 0.9362] for ACI across 2024,
  where static ICP falls to 0.7966 in its worst month.
